Frequently Asked Questions

How will the event work?

There will be two cube drafts that will affect your Swiss ranking, feeding into a Top 8 draft in the evening. Players trophying (3-0) a pod will receive a bonus match point that will also contribute to their Swiss ranking. During the Top 8 draft, there will be open drafting that does not affect tournament standings.

What will entry and prizing look like?

Entry is $70. The event will cap at 96 players. Prizing will include but is not limited to:

  • Champion: Top 8 prizing and a special altered card commemorating the tournament (details to follow)
  • Top 8: Shiny trophy pin
How do I get to the event?

The venue is just a short walk from both the Foggy Bottom/GWU and Dupont Circle Metro stations.
